# Lock-screen artwork notices

## Fingerprint vector

`fingerprint_sensor.svg` is a direct SVG format conversion of
`jaydencyril67/ScreenLock/app/src/main/res/drawable/ic_s20_fingerprint.xml`.
The vector path, 24 × 24 viewport, fill colour, stroke, and rounded stroke joins
are retained; only the Android VectorDrawable wrapper was converted to SVG for
Godot.

The source artwork is derived from Google Material Design Icons and is
