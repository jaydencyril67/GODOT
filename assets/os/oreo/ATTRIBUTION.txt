DigitalWorld Android 8 Oreo-style icon atlas

These vector icons were created specifically for the DigitalWorld project and are bundled locally for offline use. They use an original, simplified Android Oreo-era round launcher-icon treatment. They are not extracted from Android firmware, Google Pixel applications, or third-party APKs, and are not official Google or Android artwork.

The assets are authored as SVG and may be modified within this project.
