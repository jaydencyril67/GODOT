DigitalWorld Android 8 (Oreo) app icon assets

The standard Android app icons in this folder are unchanged PNG artwork from the Google Icons icon pack, release v3 (2018), by Amir Zaidi:
https://github.com/amirzaidi/GoogleIcons/tree/v3

The upstream pack's appfilter maps these assets to Android application components such as the AOSP dialer, messaging, camera, gallery, browser, calendar, contacts, clock, calculator, downloads, files, mail, notes, and settings apps. The project does not declare an explicit license; original artwork and all related rights remain with their respective owners. Confirm usage rights before distributing builds containing these images.

DigitalWorld-only app catalog entries without a matching stock Android 8 app icon reuse unchanged image files from MaterialOS Contributors' Android Icon Pack:
https://github.com/materialos/Android-Icon-Pack
The MaterialOS source states CC BY 4.0; attribution: MaterialOS Contributors.
No icons in this folder are drawn, synthesized, or generated by DigitalWorld. Images are bundled locally for offline use.
